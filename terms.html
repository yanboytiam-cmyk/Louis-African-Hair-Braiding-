1 Booking & Deposits

A deposit of 30% of the estimated service total is required to confirm and secure your appointment. This deposit is applied toward your final service balance and is non-transferable to other services or stylists.

Deposit applied to total: The 30% deposit you pay at booking is deducted from your final invoice on the day of service. You only pay the remaining 70% at your appointment.

2 Cancellations

We understand plans change. To cancel without penalty, please provide at least 48 hours' notice before your scheduled appointment time.

  • Cancellations with 48+ hours notice: full deposit refunded or applied to a rescheduled appointment
  • Cancellations with less than 48 hours notice: 50% of the deposit is forfeited to compensate the stylist for reserved time
  • Rescheduling with 48+ hours notice is always welcome and free of charge

To cancel or reschedule, contact us at hello@louisbraiding.com or call +1 681-283-9575.

3 No-Shows

Failing to attend a scheduled appointment without any prior notice constitutes a no-show. In this case:

No-show policy: The full deposit is forfeited. Stylist time and resources reserved cannot be recovered, and no refund will be issued. Repeated no-shows may result in being required to pay in full at the time of future bookings.

If you experience an emergency, please contact us as soon as possible — we handle these situations on a case-by-case basis with compassion.

4 Service Adjustments

We reserve the right to adjust pricing if the service requested changes significantly from what was originally booked. This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Significant change in style, length, or complexity at the time of appointment
  • Hair condition requiring substantially more time or product than expected
  • Requests for additional services not included in the original booking

Any pricing adjustments will be communicated and agreed upon with the client before additional work begins. We will never charge more without your explicit approval.

5 Hair Preparation

Clients are responsible for arriving with properly prepared hair unless a wash-and-prep add-on was booked in advance.

  • Hair should be freshly washed, detangled, and free from heavy product buildup
  • Avoid applying oils or heavy conditioners 48 hours prior to your appointment
  • Blown-out or stretched natural hair is preferred for most braiding styles
If additional preparation is needed on arrival, we will accommodate when possible — however, additional time and service charges may apply. This will be discussed transparently before work begins.

6 Product Use

We use professional-grade, salon-quality products throughout all services. Our team is trained in safe application practices and uses tools appropriate for each hair type and style.

It is the client's responsibility to inform us of any known allergies, scalp sensitivities, or product reactions at the time of booking or during the consultation. Louis African Hair Braiding cannot be held responsible for reactions to products if sensitivities were not disclosed in advance.

If you prefer specific products or brands, please inform us prior to your appointment — we will do our best to accommodate reasonable requests.

7 Limitation of Liability

Louis African Hair Braiding is not liable for pre-existing hair or scalp damage, breakage resulting from conditions present before the service, or reactions to products when sensitivities were not disclosed prior to service.

Our stylists take every precaution to protect the health and integrity of your hair. We recommend a consultation for clients with significantly damaged, chemically treated, or fragile hair before scheduling intensive styling services.

Our commitment: While we limit liability for pre-existing conditions, we stand fully behind the quality of our work. If you are unsatisfied with your service, contact us within 48 hours and we will work to make it right.
